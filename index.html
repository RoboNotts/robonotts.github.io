About RoboNotts

Where Students
Build Tomorrow

RoboNotts is the University of Nottingham's premier robotics team, bringing together students from Computer Science, Engineering, and beyond to tackle real-world challenges through intelligent autonomous systems.

From racing circuits to hospital wards and disaster zones, our three specialised teams push the boundaries of what student-built robots can do — competing at international competitions and advancing the state of the art.
